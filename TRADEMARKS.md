# AKFES identity policy

The source license does not grant rights to the AKFES name, logo, official
service domains, code-signing identity, or release-signing identity.

Unmodified builds may say “built from AKFES source.” Modified distributions
must use a distinct name and icon, state that they are unofficial, and must not
imply endorsement by Smarttiger2338 or the official AKFES service.

Only artifacts published by the repository owner through the protected release
process are official AKFES releases. A fork cannot use its own client build to
replace files on the official server or distribute updates to official clients.
